Our Story

A healthier world starts with
understanding

Clinical Lens CIC was founded by a UK-registered healthcare professional and filmmaker who saw a critical gap: health information was often complex, inaccessible, and not reaching the people who needed it most.

Our Founder

Bridging healthcare
and storytelling

Our founder brings a unique combination of clinical experience working within the NHS and a deep passion for filmmaking and visual storytelling. This rare combination drives everything Clinical Lens CIC does.

Having worked directly with patients from all walks of life — including migrants and communities with limited health literacy — the need for accessible, culturally appropriate health content became undeniable.

Founder, Clinical Lens CIC
UK Registered Healthcare Professional
NHS Clinician & Filmmaker
NHS Registered Filmmaker Health Educator Community Advocate
Our Mission

What drives us

01

Improve health literacy

We create content that translates medical knowledge into language and formats that everyone can understand and act on, regardless of education level.

02

Reduce health inequalities

We specifically design our content to reach underserved communities — including migrants and those who face systemic barriers to healthcare information.

03

Use creativity to educate

We believe that film and animation are powerful tools for health education — not just supplements, but primary vehicles for meaningful change.

04

Partner with purpose

We work collaboratively with the NHS, local authorities, and community organisations to ensure our work is grounded in real need and real impact.

Our Approach

Clinical insight meets creative vision

Clinically Accurate

All content is developed with clinical oversight, ensuring information is medically sound and aligned with current NHS guidance and best practice.

Community-Centred

We involve communities throughout our production process — from consultation to distribution — ensuring content resonates with those it is designed to reach.

Evidence-Based Impact

We measure our work's effectiveness and continuously improve, using audience feedback and health outcome data to refine our approach.
